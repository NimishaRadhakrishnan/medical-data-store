SRI NACHIYA MEDICALS — BILLING AND STOCK
=========================================

To start:      double-click start.bat
Then open:     http://localhost:8123   (it opens by itself)

Everything is on THIS computer. No internet is needed.

Your data:     data\shop.db
Your backups:  data\backups\
               A copy is saved when the app starts, every evening, and
               whenever you press "Back up now" in Settings.

Leave the black window open while the shop is working. Closing it stops
billing. If it is closed by mistake, double-click start.bat again — nothing
is lost.

LABELS
------
When you add a delivery, print a label for each box and stick it on before
putting the box away. At the counter, press "Scan with the camera" and hold
the box up — the medicine, batch, expiry and price go onto the bill together.

A plain USB webcam (about Rs. 600) is all you need. Use Chrome or Edge;
Safari and Firefox cannot use the camera here.

No camera? Type the first few letters of the name instead. Everything works
the same way.

If anything looks wrong, open Settings and press "Check the figures".
